HOW TO PLAY

Run. Choose. Jump!

  1. Keep jogging. Jog in place to keep your athlete moving. Stopping lets rivals catch up.
  2. Read, then choose a lane. A, B and C match the three answers. Hop sideways to switch one lane.
  3. Watch the pulse. When “Jump!” expands, jump straight up over the hurdle to answer.
  4. Race together. Shared lanes and split screen both keep your opponents visible.
Keyboard controls

P1: ← → · Space jump · R jog
P2: A D · W jump · S jog
P3: J L · I jump · K jog
P4: F H · T jump · G jog

For camera play, stand far enough back to show your full body. Tracking loss pauses the race.
